Kindling is a storytelling and organic-marketing studio that helps founders and builders find the story at the heart of their company. The brand is warm, editorial and a little cinematic: dark chocolate ink on near-white paper with a brand white of `#FFF5E5`, one orange accent, sharp corners, film-look photography stamped with a timecode.

## Content fundamentals

- Speak to the founder as **you**; Kindling is **we**. "You build your company. We'll tell your story."
- Write headlines as two beats: a plain statement in Syne, then the turn in Texturina italic `orange`. "Organic marketing matters. Few know *what to do next*."
- Use sentence case for headlines and card titles ("Pick your angle", "Refine GTM"). Use Title Case for nav links, buttons and offer names ("The Model", "Talk to Us", "Targeted Ads").
- Keep body copy to short declaratives, two to three lines: "Learn what lands. Feed what works."
- Set captions and stage labels in uppercase Roboto Mono with slashes: `KINDLING / STORY 014`, `IDEATION`, `CRAFT STORY`. The house caption is four lines: story, timecode, role, place.
- Never use emoji, exclamation marks or urgency ("Don't miss out").
- Say "story" and "founder" often; avoid "solutions", "leverage" and "synergy".

## Visual foundations

- **Ground.** Pages sit on `paper` (`#FAFAFA`) with a faint 1px `hairline` grid drawn behind the content, corner brackets framing the hero and footer strip, and ruler ticks along the left edge. Warm panels and light text on dark use `cream`, the brand white; `white` is its alias.
- **Ink and accent.** All text is `ink` (`#381105`), a warm dark brown, never pure black. `orange` is the only accent: the primary button fill, the italic phrase, chevron markers, card titles and mono index numerals. `amber` (`#FF9E37`), `olive` (`#D4C284`) and the tint steps are supporting colours for blocks and charts; never text.
- **Legibility.** `orange` (`#FF6800`) on `paper` is 2.78:1 and `white` (`#FFF5E5`) on `orange` is 2.69:1. The comps use both, so keep orange to display sizes, fills and markers, and set small text in `ink` (5.8:1 on `orange`, 16.1:1 on `paper`). On `ink` grounds `orange` is 5.8:1 and safe for text.
- **Type.** Syne for display (`display-xl`, `display-lg`, `display-md`), Instrument Sans for body and UI (`lead`, `body`, `ui`, `small`), Texturina for the italic accent (`accent-italic`); the logo is artwork, never typeset, Roboto Mono for image captions and labels (`mono-caption`, `mono-label`). All four load from Google Fonts. Text sizes are estimated from the comps assuming a 1440px frame.
- **Corners and edges.** Every corner is square (`radius-none`). Outlines are 1px (`border-hairline`) in `ink` or `orange`. There are no shadows and no gradients. The one cut shape is the photo chamfer: the top-right corner clipped on a diagonal.
- **Layout.** Centre the wordmark in the nav. Hero copy sits left, overlapping photography that steps down to the right. Feature rows are three equal columns with `space-6` between them. Sections breathe with `space-8` to `space-9` of padding.
- **Imagery.** Warm, saturated, film-grain photographs of real people at work and in motion, framed in portrait, each carrying the mono caption bottom-left. See `imagery.md`.
- **Motion.** None is specified. Keep transitions to simple colour changes on hover.
- **Focus.** A 2px solid `ink` outline with a 2px offset (`border-strong`), 16:1 on `paper`.

## Iconography

Kindling uses no icon set. The only glyph is the chevron marker: an elongated right-pointing triangle in `orange`, used as a list bullet, as the flags above a numbered photo. It is drawn in the `Marker` inside the bundle; the source mark was not supplied as a file, so it is redrawn from the comps. Never use emoji or a third-party icon font.

## Logo

Use `assets/Logos/kindling-wordmark.png` (hand-cut wordmark) and `kindling-monogram.png` (the K) in `ink` on light grounds; the `-cream` files reverse them for `ink`, `orange` and dark photography. Components carry the same artwork through `Logo`. Keep clear space of one K-height around it, never retype the name in a font, never recolour it to `orange`. The wordmark sits centred in the nav bar and closes the stage rail on photography. The files are transparent PNGs; ask for the vector original for print or very large sizes.
