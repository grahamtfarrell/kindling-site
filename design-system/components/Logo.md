# Logo

The Kindling logo as supplied: the hand-cut wordmark (`variant="wordmark"`, the default) and the single-letter K monogram (`variant="monogram"`). `tone="ink"` is the `ink` brown (#381105) for `paper`, `cream` and light photography; `tone="cream"` is the brand white (#FFF5E5) for `ink` grounds, `orange` fills and dark photography. `height` sets the size in px; the width follows.

Use the wordmark in the nav bar (centred, 40px tall), in the stage rail and as the sign-off on full-bleed imagery. Use the monogram where the wordmark would fall below 24px tall: avatars, favicons, stamps.

Do: keep clear space of one K-height on every side; place it on grounds where it holds at least 4.5:1 (the `ink` tone on `paper` is 16.1:1, the `cream` tone on `ink` is 15.6:1). Don't: retype the name in a font, stretch or rotate the mark, add outlines or shadows, or recolour it to `orange`, whose contrast on `paper` is 2.78:1.

The images are raster (transparent PNG); the wordmark's speckled counters are part of the artwork. Request the original vector file for print and very large sizes.
