# NavBar

The top bar: page links at the left, the Kindling logo (wordmark, `ink` tone, 40px tall) centred, then a plain "Log in" link and the primary Button at the right. Three equal grid columns keep the wordmark on the centre line at every width.

Consumer provides: `links` as `[{label, href}]` (three or fewer, Title Case: "The Model", "The Work", "The Who"), and optional `login` and `cta` labels. The logo is built in; never replace it with typeset text.

Do: sit it on `paper` with the section hairline grid behind it. Don't: add a bottom border, shadow or sticky blur; the bar has no container of its own.
