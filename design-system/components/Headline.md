# Headline

The signature two-voice headline: plain words in Syne (`display-xl`, or `display-md` with `size="md"`) followed by a closing phrase in Texturina italic, coloured `orange` (`accent-italic`). "You build your company. We'll *tell your story*." and "Organic marketing matters. Few know *what to do next*."

Consumer provides: the plain sentence as `children` and the closing phrase as `accent`. Write in sentence case, second person, short declaratives; the accent phrase is the promise or the turn, three to four words, lowercase, ending the headline (a full stop may follow in `ink`). `as` picks the tag (`h1` default). On photography set the whole headline in `white` at `display-md` and drop the accent colour.

Do: use one accent phrase per headline. Don't: italicise anything in Syne, or set the accent in bold. Orange at display size on `paper` is 2.78:1 (the comps' own pairing); on an `ink` ground it is 5.8:1.
