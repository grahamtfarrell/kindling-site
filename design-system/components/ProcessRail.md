# ProcessRail

A single line of stage labels laid across a full-bleed photograph, in Roboto Mono uppercase (`mono-label`), all `white`, ending with the wordmark at the far right: IDEATION, PLAN, EXECUTE, CRAFT STORY, RESULTS. It spaces its children evenly across the container.

Consumer provides: `steps` (four to six one- or two-word labels) over a dark or mid-tone photograph; the rail is white and needs a ground of at least 4.5:1 against white, so set it on the darker part of the image or add an `ink` scrim. Don't use it on `paper`.
