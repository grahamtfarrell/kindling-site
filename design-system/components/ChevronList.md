# ChevronList

A horizontal run of short offer words, each led by the `orange` chevron marker, in Instrument Sans 500 (`ui`): "Storytelling", "Content", "Targeted Ads". It sits under the hero buttons.

Consumer provides: `items` as short Title Case nouns, three or four at most. The marker is the only bullet in the system; use it for lists, never a dot or check. It wraps to a new line rather than shrinking.
